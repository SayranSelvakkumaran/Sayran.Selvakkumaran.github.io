Total artificial heart

Team Bath Heart, 2026 to now

I'm part of a student team designing an artificial heart. I use FEA to see how the design behaves under load, find where it's constrained, and feed that back into the next iteration. That includes modelling the hyperelastic materials involved.

I'm also helping write the technical report for the 2026 Heart Hackathon, which means turning analysis and research into decisions other people can follow.

Tools: ANSYS, MATLAB, CAD

Robotic coating maintenance for small modular reactors

AtkinsRéalis, placement year

I led a feasibility project on using robots to maintain coatings for small modular reactors. It went through three gated reviews: first a feasibility study, then Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis to compare the options, and finally a proposed robotic operating process.

Skills: robotics, MCDA, feasibility studies

3D-printed robotic hand

A working hand driven by an Arduino, with fishing wire pulling each finger. It copies the movement of the user's own hand.

Small modular reactor outreach kit

A 3D-printed demonstration kit about small modular reactors. I use it in school outreach to get children interested in engineering.

Weeding tool for reduced grip strength

A gardening tool for people with limited grip strength and mobility. I worked on the ergonomics, cut the force needed to use it, and checked it wouldn't fail in use.

Forklift drive shaft optimisation

Stress analysis of a forklift drive shaft under dynamic loading. I compared materials and manufacturing methods against the structural and production requirements.

Automated cell-packing machine

A machine concept built from several mechanical sub-systems, with a plant layout and an operational storyboard. I assessed the risks using UML Activity Diagrams, Fault Tree Analysis and FMECA.
